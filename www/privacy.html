Privacy policy

Plant Pals · houseplant watering tracker

Last updated 27 November 2026

In short: Plant Pals doesn’t collect, store or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no trackers. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The plants, rooms, watering history and settings you enter are saved in the app’s own storage on your device only. They aren’t sent to us or anyone else. The app works fully offline and doesn’t connect to any server.

Backups and sharing

  • Export backup creates a file of your plant data and opens your phone’s share menu. You choose where it goes, such as Google Drive, email or your Files app. Plant Pals never sees where it goes.
  • Sitter’s plan creates a text message for your plant-sitter that you share in the same way.
  • If you have Android’s own backup turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup to your Google account. That is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by Plant Pals.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only used to show your daily watering reminder, and only if you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself.
  • Run at start-up: lets the app restore your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.

The app doesn’t ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

Plant Pals is a general-audience app. It doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it doesn’t collect personal information at all.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Clear all plants in the app, or uninstall the app. Either removes the data from your phone. There’s nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds an optional sync feature, the new version will be posted here with a new date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on the project page: github.com/dayman92/plant-pals/issues.
